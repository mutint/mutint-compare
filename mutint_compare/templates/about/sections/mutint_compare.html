Compare pivots a whole experiment into one table: every mutation is a row, every sequenced sample a column, and each cell carries the frequency at which that mutation was called in that sample. It is the view for asking which mutations recur across a population, as opposed to the per-sample Mutations page, which shows one sample's calls in breseq's own column order.

Its Show menu narrows the rows to the convergent mutations — a gene hit in at least so many populations, or so large a fraction of them — or the fixed ones, present at both of the last two time points sampled from at least so many populations. Both thresholds are yours to set, and both sets are computed from the rows as you have filtered them. Export writes either the rows showing or all of them.

It stores nothing. The table is mutint-core's mutation matrix, the same one behind Search: breseq's own columns for each mutation, a frequency cell per sample, and menus to choose which samples, columns and types show — choices that follow you from experiment to experiment. A deployment that removes this plugin loses the page and nothing else.
