Compare pivots a whole experiment into one table: every mutation is a row, every sequenced sample a column, and each cell carries the frequency at which that mutation was called in that sample. It is the view for asking which mutations recur across a population, as opposed to the per-sample Mutations page, which shows one sample's calls in breseq's own column order.

It computes nothing and stores nothing. The table is mutint-core's mutation matrix, the same one behind Search, Fixed Mutations and Converged Mutations: breseq's own columns for each mutation, a frequency cell per sample, and two menus to choose which samples and which columns show — choices that follow you from experiment to experiment. A deployment that removes this plugin loses the page and nothing else.
